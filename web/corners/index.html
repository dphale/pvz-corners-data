      Sources

      • TIMS/SWITRS data — 2015–2024 are final TIMS/SWITRS data. 2025 and 2026 are provisional and can change (TIMS, 28 September 2026).
      • LA City Council map — Los Angeles City Council districts adopted in 2021, Ordinance 187279, effective 10 December 2021, Bureau of Engineering.
      • Census data — U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Census Redistricting Data (P.L. 94-171), total population for city rates. Council-district populations are the city-adjusted counts in Attachment A, not raw Census counts.
      • Boundaries — City limits for cities within Los Angeles County, and Los Angeles County school-district areas, from Los Angeles County GIS Political Boundaries.
      • PVZ Crosswalks — People's Vision Zero records of crosswalks painted.
      • Schools — LA County GIS TK-12 campus polygons (Infrastructure MapServer layer 2, Schools). Open public and charter names from California Public Schools 2025-26.
      • Parks — City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks park boundaries.
      • HIN (2024) — LADOT High Injury Network (2024), NavigateLA layer 172.
      • Safety Corridors (2024) — LADOT Safety Corridors (2024), NavigateLA layer 173.
      • Mobility Plan 2035 — LADOT Mobility Plan 2035 networks (HLA Dashboard Web Map Live layer 4).
